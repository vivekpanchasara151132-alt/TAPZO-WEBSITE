Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

1. What is an NFC Smart Card?
An NFC (Near Field Communication) Smart Card is a durable PVC card with an embedded microchip that wirelessly transmits your business link or digital landing page when tapped against a compatible smartphone.
2. How does TAPZO work?
A customer simply taps their smartphone on your TAPZO card or scans the printed QR code. Your configured Google Review link or personalized digital landing page opens immediately in their browser.
3. Do customers need to download an app?
No. No special customer app is required for standard web links. Modern Android and iOS smartphones read NFC tags and QR codes natively and open links directly in the web browser.
4. Can customers scan the QR code?
Yes. Every TAPZO card includes a precision-printed QR code alongside the NFC chip so customers with older devices or NFC turned off can scan and connect effortlessly.
5. Can I add my Instagram and WhatsApp links?
Yes. Both TAPZO Premium (₹799) and TAPZO Pro (₹1,299) include Instagram, WhatsApp Chat, Facebook, and Google Review links on your personalized digital landing page.
6. Which package should I choose?
Choose TAPZO Basic (₹499) if you only want a direct Google Rating & Review card. Choose TAPZO Premium (₹799) for social links, WhatsApp, and a custom logo landing page. Choose TAPZO Pro (₹1,299) if you also need your website, YouTube, LinkedIn, Google Maps, email, and full contact details.
7. Can I customize my card?
Yes. Basic includes your custom business name on a professional card layout, while Premium and Pro include your custom business logo and personalized digital landing page.
